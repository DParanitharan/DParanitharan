# Diabetes Risk Prediction

**Machine Learning Project in R (DSA1101)** · Aug 2023 – Nov 2023

## Goal
Predict diabetes risk across a large survey population and identify the variables that matter most.

## Approach
- Modelled diabetes risk across **70,692 respondents**.
- Reduced features from **21 → 13 variables**.
- Benchmarked **4 classifiers** using **5-fold cross-validation** and **ROC/AUC**.

## Stack
R · classification models · 5-fold CV · ROC/AUC
