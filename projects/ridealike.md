# Ridealike — Serverless Verification & Dynamic Pricing

*Data Science Specialist Intern (Full-time)* · May 2024 – Aug 2024

## Context
Ridealike is a car-sharing marketplace. Two bottlenecks: manual identity verification of users, and static pricing that ignored the competitive market.

## What I Did
- **Architected and deployed a Dockerised serverless user-verification pipeline on AWS** (Lambda, S3, EventBridge, Textract, Rekognition) with **MongoDB**, automating manual identity checks for **50+ users weekly**.
- **Built a serverless dynamic-pricing backend** using **Selenium** competitor scraping and **SageMaker** ML models.
- **Analysed company data in R** for investor pitch-deck visualisations, and **led operations for 100+ trips**.

## Stack
AWS (Lambda, S3, EventBridge, Textract, Rekognition, SageMaker) · MongoDB · Docker · Selenium · R

## Impact
Removed a manual verification bottleneck and gave the business market-aware pricing — both running serverless.
