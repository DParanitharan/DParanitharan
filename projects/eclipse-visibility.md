# Eclipse Visibility Analysis

**Data Visualisation Project in R (DSA2101)** · Aug 2024 – Nov 2024

## Goal
Turn open eclipse-observation data into clear spatial maps of US eclipse visibility.

## Approach
- Cleaned and processed **64,000+ observations** from **four open TidyTuesday datasets**.
- Produced **spatial maps** of US eclipse visibility using **ggplot2**, **sf**, and **rnaturalearth**.

## Stack
R · ggplot2 · sf · rnaturalearth

## Outcome
A reproducible visualisation workflow that communicates eclipse-path visibility across the US at a glance.
