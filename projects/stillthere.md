# StillThere? — Building Change Verification

**Client Project for Nippon Koei Business Partners (DSA3101)** · *Product Manager* · Jan 2026 – Apr 2026

## Problem
Infrastructure and disaster-response records go stale: a building logged in a database may no longer exist on the ground. StillThere? verifies whether recorded buildings are still present by checking them against current satellite imagery.

## What We Built
A **full-stack AI platform** that integrates the **Segment Anything Model (SAM)** to verify **1,228 buildings** against up-to-date satellite imagery, achieving **87% validation accuracy**.

## My Role — Product Manager
- Owned the **sprint roadmap**, prioritising features against client needs and team velocity.
- Bridged the client (Nippon Koei Business Partners) and the engineering team, translating requirements into shippable increments.

## Stack
React · Django · PostGIS · Segment Anything Model (SAM)

## Outcome
A working platform that automates a previously manual verification step, with measured accuracy the client could trust.
